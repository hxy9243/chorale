# Baroque Style Profile: Johann Sebastian Bach (1685–1750)

## 1. Musical Characteristics

1. **Contrapuntal Motor**:
   - Constant, unbroken rhythmic drive. Continuous streams of eighth or sixteenth notes without abrupt pauses.
   - Motoric forward momentum with all voices maintaining melodic independence.

2. **Harmonic Language**:
   - Circle-of-fifths sequences (e.g. `vi -> ii -> V -> I`).
   - Minor keys: extensive use of harmonic minor (raised $\hat{7}$) and melodic minor (raised $\hat{6}$ and $\hat{7}$ ascending, natural descending).
   - Secondary dominants (`V/V`, `V/iv`) and diminished seventh chords (`vii°7`) used as emotional focal points.
   - Suspensions on strong beats: 4–3, 7–6, and 9–8 with explicit consonant preparation and stepwise downward resolution.

3. **Bass Line & Voice Leading**:
   - Continuous walking bass (often in continuous eighth notes) providing harmonic momentum.
   - Strict avoidance of parallel fifths and octaves.
   - Upper voice spacing (Soprano to Alto, Alto to Tenor) should remain within one octave.

---

## 2. Accompaniment & Textural Guidelines

- Strict 2-part, 3-part, or 4-part polyphony.
- In two-part keyboard writing (inventions), the two voices engage in close imitation, invertible counterpoint, and motives exchanged across hands.
- In four-part chorale writing, homorhythmic SATB with linear voice-leading and cadential fermatas.
