# Chorale score workflow

1. Resolve the score, then read its summary and the exact written measures before making musical claims.
2. Cite written measure ranges and voice scope. Separate facts in the score from stylistic interpretation.
3. Use bounded proposals for annotations and edits. Keep the base revision from the read and surface conflicts instead of retrying blindly.
4. After a mutation, re-read the affected range and confirm the resulting revision.
5. If the score page is unavailable, use the data tools; do not claim playback or visual confirmation occurred.
